# Converged paper example

These files were generated by `examples/paper_example.jl` for the paper
parameters at `p/q=1/20`, using `nactive=6`, reciprocal cutoff `lg=9`, and
magnetic-translation cutoff `smax=5`.  The momentum mesh is unchanged from the
main-text calculation; the improvement comes from the basis and magnetic
translation truncations.

Key results:

- target magnetic subband: 79;
- correction frame: `(79, 78)`;
- Chern number: 1;
- residual integrated trace / trace-condition excess:
  `2.2469106188858858e-4`;
- integrated ideal fraction: `0.9999642405856133`;
- Fig. 4 target weight within the central zero-field pair:
  `0.919354982036541`;
- Fig. 4 C3-averaged K-region weight relative to the map maximum:
  `0.12760522509466157`.

For comparison, the otherwise identical `lg=7, smax=3` rebuild gives
`eta=2.337843720059746e-3` and ideal fraction `0.9996280590073517`.  The
Fig. 4 central-pair weight remains stable (`0.9192773869978659`), showing that
the physical component distribution is robust while the intrinsic geometric
residual benefits substantially from the stricter truncation.

The large `magnetic_hw.jls` cache is intentionally omitted.  It is regenerated
by the example script; all lightweight numerical tables and the final Fig. 4
projection are included here.
